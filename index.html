The Gold Guide

What every buyer in India should know before they buy gold.

01

Look for the hallmark

Since 2021, gold jewellery sold in India must carry a BIS hallmark with three marks: the BIS logo, the purity (e.g. 22K916) and a 6-character HUID that's unique to that piece.

Tip: type the HUID into the government's BIS CARE app to verify it yourself.

02

Know your karats

KaratMarkGoldBest for
24K99999.9%Coins & bars
22K91691.6%Traditional & bridal
18K75075.0%Diamond & gemstone
14K58558.5%Light daily wear
03

How the price is made

Gold rate × weight+Making charges+Stones (if any)+3% GST

Making charges cover the craftsmanship — intricate handwork costs more than plain machine-made pieces. Ask for the bill to show each part separately.

Try the price estimator →
04

Caring for your jewellery

  • Put jewellery on last — after perfume, lotion and hairspray.
  • Store pieces separately in soft cloth so they don't scratch.
  • Take it off for swimming, the gym and housework.
  • Bring it in for a professional clean and check — it's on us.

    Frequently Asked

    Is your gold hallmarked?

    Yes. Our gold jewellery is BIS-hallmarked and carries a 6-character HUID that you can verify on the BIS CARE app.

    Can I exchange my old gold?

    Yes. We test your old gold in front of you, agree its value at the day's rate, and adjust it against your new purchase. Use the old gold estimator for a rough idea before you come.

    Do you make custom or bridal designs?

    Yes — bring a photo or idea and we'll work with you on the design, weight and budget. Book a consultation so we can set aside time.

    Why don't you show prices online?

    Gold prices change every day and every piece has a slightly different weight. Send us a WhatsApp with the piece you like and we'll reply with today's exact price.

    Can I see jewellery without visiting?

    Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll show you pieces on a video call.
